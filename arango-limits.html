ArangoDB Limitations & Fly.io Costs ⚠️

What you need to know before migrating your RAG architecture.

The Trade-offs of a Multi-Model Engine

While ArangoDB dramatically simplifies the JEV architecture by merging Graphs and Vectors into a single database, it is not a silver bullet. There are three primary limitations you must consider:

1. Memory Hungry

ArangoSearch (the vector search engine) relies heavily on keeping indices in RAM. It is not as lightweight as Qdrant or SQLite. You cannot reliably run ArangoDB on edge micro-VMs with 256MB of RAM.

2. The AQL Learning Curve

Unlike Neo4j which uses Cypher (the industry standard for graphs), ArangoDB uses its own ArangoDB Query Language (AQL). If your LLM agents are trained exclusively to generate Cypher, you will need to update your LangChain prompts.

3. Ecosystem Integration

The LangChain ecosystem heavily favors Pinecone, Qdrant, and Neo4j. While ArangoDB is supported, you may find fewer cutting-edge community tutorials and pre-built LangChain wrapper classes.

Running on Fly.io: Can you do it for free?

Fly.io offers a generous "Hobby Tier" allowance that includes up to three `shared-cpu-1x` VMs with 256MB of RAM, and 3GB of persistent volume storage for free. However, ArangoDB cannot reliably run on the free tier.

Fly.io Resource Requirement for ArangoDB Estimated Monthly Cost
Compute (RAM) Minimum 2GB RAM (`shared-cpu-1x`). ArangoSearch will OOM kill (Out of Memory) on 256MB. ~$5.70 / month
Storage (Persistent Volume) You will need at least 10GB to store 100k Markdown files and their 768-dimensional embeddings. ~$1.50 / month
Network (`.flycast`) Internal IPv6 routing within your Fly.io organization. $0.00 (Free)

The Final Cost Verdict: ~$7.20 / Month

While it is not completely free like a local SQLite/Chroma setup, running an enterprise-grade Multi-Model database (Graph + Vector) globally on Fly.io for under $10 a month is an exceptionally cost-effective way to host your Second Brain.
