The Evolution of the Second Brain

What happens when you add JEV to 100,000 files?

Before JEV

  • 1
    Information Black Hole Notes go into Google Drive but are rarely found again. Keyword search is too rigid.
  • 2
    LLM Hallucinations Pasting partial notes into ChatGPT leads to made-up connections because the LLM lacks the full 100k-file context.
  • 3
    Isolated Thoughts A note about "Marketing" and a note about "Psychology" never interact, missing key insights.
  • 4
    No Graph Awareness Cannot answer questions like "Who was the person connected to the Alpha project in 2021?"

After JEV

  • 1
    Semantic Retrieval (Qdrant) Search by concept, not just keywords. Instant retrieval of highly relevant paragraphs out of 100k files.
  • 2
    Grounded LLM Responses LangChain dynamically feeds the exact 5 relevant context nodes to the LLM. Zero hallucination.
  • 3
    Automated Synthesis JEV actively cross-pollinates ideas by running similarity scores on disparate folders overnight.
  • 4
    Graph Relationships (Neo4j) Understands complex multi-hop links between authors, dates, projects, and concepts automatically.
